Not sure which number is which?

Total bill amount — look for "Final Bill", "Gross Amount" or "Total Bill Amount" on the hospital bill or the TPA settlement letter.

Amount paid at the counter — look for "Amount Payable by Patient" or "Net Amount Paid" on the discharge summary or payment receipt.

Hospital discount — look for "MOU Discount", "PPN Discount" or "Package Discount" on the bill. Leave as 0 if you don't see one.

Co-pay percentage — check your policy schedule, or "Co-Payment %" on the claim settlement letter. Try 0 first if you can't find it.

Different insurers and hospitals word these differently — this is a guide, not an exact match for every document.

Who this is for

Built around one fully documented real cashless settlement — and meant for moments like these:

  • Checking what was charged after your own or a family member's hospital stay
  • A senior citizen going through claim papers without help nearby
  • A parent making sense of a child's hospital bill

These are illustrative situations, not additional validated cases — the one documented result above is the only outcome ClearBill claims.
